A parallel that isn't catalogued in wear-m3-catalog yet is flagged unpaired. Components with no parallel on either side are listed below the table. The leading M3 Wear OS kit column is the published design reference BOTH implementations are reproducing, which is what lets a divergence be attributed to one of them rather than merely observed between them. It is contributed by whichever catalog carries the figma: mapping; rows where neither does read "no kit reference".
